# Referral scheme and terms update — 29 October 2026

Each active qualifying referral gives 25 percentage points off subsequent core monthly subscription payments, capped at 100%. The discount continues into reporting after FCA authorisation on the same subscription. The initial £100 payment remains payable. Separate subscriptions, separately charged services and third-party charges remain outside the scheme.

A referral must be identified, accepted, onboarded and have its first subscription payment received. It must remain subscribed and up to date with payments. If it cancels or ceases to qualify, only that referral's discount ends from the next invoice (Terms clause 8.4).

| Active qualifying referrals | Discount | Monthly amount on £100 base |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0% | £100 |
| 1 | 25% | £75 |
| 2 | 50% | £50 |
| 3 | 75% | £25 |
| 4 | 100% | £0 |
