/when-is-ccr009-due · Checked 2026-11-12 · Official-source inconsistency, explicitly qualified in the approved text

Exact approved wording inspected
At exactly £5 million, the ongoing Handbook rule uses “£5 million or less” for annual reporting, but the FCA overview uses “£5m or more” for twice-yearly reporting. Note 12(g) and (h) also overlap at that boundary for firms entering scope in January–June from 2026. Ask the FCA to confirm the applicable schedule if this affects your firm; this guide does not resolve that official inconsistency.

Ongoing Handbook rules separate at/below £5m from above £5m and specify January–June plus January–December for larger firms. The FCA overview includes the exact boundary in the larger group; first-period paragraphs (g)/(h) also overlap there. The guide says to seek FCA confirmation for affected firms. Retain this qualification; do not silently pick an interpretation.
