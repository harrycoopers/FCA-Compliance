Of those anchors, 32 lead to guides and five to services. Expanded regression and rendered-browser checks; added a repeatable original-versus-final performance comparison. No new runtime dependency or animation initialization.

The six FCA guides show the current offer after the last educational section; eight reporting guides show the scoped CCR009 offer there; four ongoing/hub guides have no FCA offer. All 18 retain one closing animated booking CTA.

Existing strengths were already present before this task: server-rendered content, one H1, descriptive section headings, unique metadata, canonical URLs, index directives, sitemap inclusion, Article markup, Open Graph article type, source links and a connected guide directory. These are verified strengths, not newly created SEO fixes. ## Research method and page-by-page assessment

Observed **12 November 2026**. Queries use UK audience/regulatory terminology; the search tool does not establish controlled UK Google geolocation or fixed rankings. Sources include the FCA and credible trade/professional/dealer-support publishers. Competitors were reviewed as evidence of page formats and user tasks, not treated as legal authority. Own-site results, unrelated acronym matches, old rule interpretations and generic service promises were not used to prove demand. Some narrow CCR009 searches were noisy and did not identify strong dedicated competing pages.

All primary purposes below are **editorial inferences informed by the observed results and existing content**. There is no Search Console evidence, search volume, ranking measurement, CTR forecast or claim of proven cannibalisation. 2. **Resolve the exact £5 million CCR009 boundary.** The [FCA CCR009 overview](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/consumer-credit-reporting/ccr009) says £5 million or more; the indexed [SUP 16 reporting table/notes](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/sup16?timeline=true) contains more-than/less-than-or-equal wording for ongoing reporting and separate first-period notes. 3. **Strengthen distinct utility where approved:** a permission decision diagram; an application evidence checklist; an annotated CCR009 field-to-record example; a downloadable final sign-off checklist; a separate preparation responsibility schedule; a worked correction scenario; website promotion examples; and a proportionate Consumer Duty monitoring/board-review example. These require new substantive material and review. Do not copy competitor templates or imply universal compliance.
4. **Editorial identity:** add genuine author/reviewer identity and relevant experience only when supplied and visibly attributable. Record actual substantive revisions before adding publication/modification dates. Existing publisher attribution is not evidence of an individual author’s credentials.
5. **Search Console:** review query-by-page impressions/clicks, actual chosen canonicals, indexing and UK/device segments for the CCR009 cluster and eligibility/permission cluster. Compare sufficiently long periods after any separately authorised deployment. Repeated queries across URLs indicate investigation, not automatic merging.
